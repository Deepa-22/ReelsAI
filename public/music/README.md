# Background Music Files

The cinematic renderer mixes background music into the downloaded video.
**These files are NOT shipped with the repo** because of licensing — you need to download and place 5 small CC0 mp3 files here.

## Required files

| File | Mood | Vibe |
|---|---|---|
| `emotional-piano.mp3` | EMOTIONAL | Soft piano, slow, vulnerable |
| `cinematic-strings.mp3` | CINEMATIC | Epic orchestral strings |
| `viral-beat.mp3` | VIRAL | Upbeat trap/pop instrumental |
| `dreamy-ambient.mp3` | DREAMY | Soft synth ambient |
| `fast-pop.mp3` | FAST_PACED | High-energy upbeat pop |

## Where to get them (all CC0 / royalty-free)

1. **Pixabay Music** — [pixabay.com/music](https://pixabay.com/music) — free for any use, no attribution required
2. **freemusicarchive.org** — CC-licensed catalog
3. **YouTube Audio Library** — free for content creators

## Tips for picking tracks

- Keep them **30-60 seconds** loopable (the renderer loops if shorter than reel)
- Avoid vocals — they fight with narration
- Pick low-energy versions — they sit *under* the voice, not over it
- Aim for **<500KB per file** (mp3 96kbps mono is plenty for background)

## What happens if files are missing

The audio mixer silently skips background music and proceeds with narration only.
Your videos still get the voice — they just don't have music underneath until you drop files here.
