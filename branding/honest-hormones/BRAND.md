# 🌸 Honest Hormones — Brand Kit

Complete visual identity for the @HonestHormones YouTube channel + Instagram + TikTok.

---

## Brand essence

**Tagline:** *Real stories. Real hormones. Honestly.*

**Voice:**
- Warm, vulnerable, never preachy
- Talks like a friend who's been there
- Uses "I" and "we" — never "you should"
- Medically accurate but jargon-free
- Soft strength — not corporate wellness

**Niche:** Women's hormonal health journey — PMOS (formerly PCOS), thyroid, perimenopause, hormonal mental health.

---

## Color palette

| Role | Hex | Usage |
|---|---|---|
| **Primary — Soft Rose** | `#E8A0B7` | Brand accent, buttons, CTA |
| **Secondary — Warm Cream** | `#F5E6D8` | Backgrounds, calm areas |
| **Deep — Aubergine** | `#3D1F2E` | Headers, contrast text |
| **Sage** | `#A8B9A0` | Wellness accents, healing imagery |
| **Soft White** | `#FAF7F4` | Main background |
| **Charcoal** | `#1A1416` | Body text on light bg |

Palette feeling: *quiet, healing, feminine without being girly, premium but accessible.*

---

## Typography

| Use | Font | Weight | Backup |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Display / Headlines** | Cormorant Garamond | 500 (Medium) | Playfair Display, serif |
| **Body / Text** | Inter | 400-500 | system-ui, sans-serif |
| **Accent / Quotes** | Caveat | 400 (handwritten feel) | cursive |

Pairing rationale: serif for warmth + clean sans for readability + occasional handwritten note for vulnerability.
